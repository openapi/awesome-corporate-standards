## Certification Bodies & Standard Organizations

> Who writes the rules? This section explains the main bodies that develop and publish international standards.

| Body | Full Name | Scope | Region |
|------|-----------|-------|--------|
| **ISO** | International Organization for Standardization | Cross-industry: quality, security, environment, safety, energy, and more | International |
| **IEC** | International Electrotechnical Commission | Electrical, electronic, and related technologies | International |
| **ITU** | International Telecommunication Union | Telecommunications and ICT | International (UN agency) |
| **IEEE** | Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers | Engineering, computing, telecommunications | International |
| **NIST** | National Institute of Standards and Technology | Cybersecurity, metrology, technology | USA |
| **ANSI** | American National Standards Institute | Voluntary consensus standards | USA |
| **BSI** | British Standards Institution | Multi-sector | UK |
| **DIN** | Deutsches Institut für Normung | Multi-sector | Germany |
| **UNI** | Ente Italiano di Normazione | Multi-sector, also publishes UNI PdR (practice references) | Italy |
| **AFNOR** | Association Française de Normalisation | Multi-sector | France |
| **CEN** | European Committee for Standardization | European harmonized standards (EN) | Europe |
| **CENELEC** | European Committee for Electrotechnical Standardization | Electrotechnical standards | Europe |
| **AICPA** | American Institute of Certified Public Accountants | Auditing, trust services (SOC) | USA |
| **PCI SSC** | Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council | Payment security | International |

## Quality Management

Standards that certify an organization's ability to consistently deliver products and services that meet customer and regulatory requirements.

- **[ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html)** — The world's most adopted quality management system (QMS) standard. Applies to any organization, any size, any industry. Certification demonstrates consistent quality processes and continuous improvement culture.
- **[ISO 9000](https://www.iso.org/standard/45481.html)** — Vocabulary and fundamental concepts for the ISO 9000 family.
- **[ISO 9004](https://www.iso.org/standard/70397.html)** — Guidance for sustained organizational success beyond ISO 9001.
- **[CMMI](https://cmmiinstitute.com/)** — Capability Maturity Model Integration. Process improvement framework for software and services organizations, with maturity levels 1–5.
- **[Six Sigma](https://www.asq.org/cert/six-sigma)** — Data-driven methodology for eliminating defects in processes (DMAIC/DMADV). Organizations can achieve Belt-level certification.
- **[IATF 16949](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/)** — Quality management for the automotive supply chain (see also [Automotive](#automotive)).

## Information Security

Standards that certify an organization's information security management system (ISMS) and practices.

- **[ISO/IEC 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)** — The global benchmark for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). Defines requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and improving information security across people, processes, and technology. Uses a risk-based approach with 93 controls in Annex A (ISO 27002).
- **[ISO/IEC 27002](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html)** — Code of practice for information security controls. Companion to 27001, not independently certifiable.
- **[ISO/IEC 27017](https://www.iso.org/standard/43757.html)** — Information security controls for cloud services.
- **[ISO/IEC 27018](https://www.iso.org/standard/76559.html)** — Protection of personally identifiable information (PII) in public clouds.
- **[ISO/IEC 27701](https://www.iso.org/standard/71670.html)** — Extension to 27001/27002 for privacy information management (PIMS). Bridges ISMS and GDPR compliance.
- **[SOC 2](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2)** — Service Organization Control 2, issued by AICPA. Audits trust service criteria: Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, Privacy. Type I = point in time, Type II = over a period.
- **[SOC 3](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-3)** — Public-facing summary report of SOC 2 findings.
- **[NIST CSF](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework)** — NIST Cybersecurity Framework. Voluntary framework for managing and reducing cybersecurity risk. Not a certification per se but widely adopted as a compliance reference.
- **[NIST SP 800-53](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final)** — Security and privacy controls for federal information systems (USA), widely referenced globally.
- **[CIS Controls](https://www.cisecurity.org/controls)** — Center for Internet Security. Prioritized set of safeguards to mitigate the most common cyberattacks.

## Environmental Management

Standards certifying an organization's environmental impact management and sustainability practices.

- **[ISO 14001](https://www.iso.org/iso-14001-environmental-management.html)** — Environmental Management System (EMS). Certifies that an organization systematically controls its environmental impact and pursues continuous improvement.
- **[ISO 14004](https://www.iso.org/standard/74342.html)** — General guidelines for EMS implementation.
- **[ISO 14040/14044](https://www.iso.org/standard/37456.html)** — Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) methodology.
- **[ISO 50001](https://www.iso.org/iso-50001-energy-management.html)** — Energy Management Systems (EnMS). Helps organizations improve energy performance, efficiency, and consumption.
- **[EMAS](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/circular-economy/emas_en)** — Eco-Management and Audit Scheme. EU voluntary instrument for organizations to evaluate, report, and improve their environmental performance. More demanding than ISO 14001.
- **[GHG Protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/)** — Greenhouse Gas Protocol. The most widely used international accounting standard for GHG emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3).

## Health & Safety

Standards certifying occupational health, safety, and well-being management systems.

- **[ISO 45001](https://www.iso.org/iso-45001-occupational-health-and-safety.html)** — Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS). Replaced OHSAS 18001 in 2018. Certifies that an organization proactively manages workplace hazards and risks.
- **[OHSAS 18001](https://www.bsigroup.com/en-GB/ohsas-18001-occupational-health-and-safety/)** — Predecessor to ISO 45001. Officially withdrawn in 2021; organizations should have migrated.
- **[ISO 45003](https://www.iso.org/standard/64283.html)** — Psychological health and safety at work. Guidance on managing psychosocial risks.

## Privacy & Data Protection

Frameworks and standards governing how organizations handle personal data.

- **[GDPR](https://gdpr.eu/)** — General Data Protection Regulation. EU regulation (2016/679). Not an ISO standard — it is binding law for any organization processing EU residents' data. No single "GDPR certification" exists, but ISO 27701 bridges it.
- **[ISO/IEC 27701](https://www.iso.org/standard/71670.html)** — See [Information Security](#information-security). The closest certifiable standard to GDPR compliance.
- **[CCPA](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa)** — California Consumer Privacy Act. US state law regulating data privacy for California residents.
- **[HIPAA](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/index.html)** — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. US federal law governing health information privacy and security.
- **[ePrivacy Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02002L0058-20091219)** — EU directive on privacy in electronic communications. Companion to GDPR.
- **[ISO 29100](https://www.iso.org/standard/45123.html)** — Privacy framework. Foundational concepts and terminology for privacy standards family.

## Financial & Accounting

Standards and frameworks governing financial reporting, controls, and auditing.

- **[SOX](https://www.sec.gov/spotlight/sarbanes-oxley.htm)** — Sarbanes-Oxley Act. US federal law mandating financial reporting controls and auditor independence for public companies.
- **[IFRS](https://www.ifrs.org/)** — International Financial Reporting Standards. Accounting standards adopted in 140+ countries.
- **[GAAP](https://asc.fasb.org/)** — Generally Accepted Accounting Principles. US accounting standard.
- **[PCI DSS](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/)** — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. Mandatory for any organization that stores, processes, or transmits cardholder data. Currently PCI DSS v4.0.
- **[ISAE 3402 / SSAE 18](https://www.iaasb.org/publications/international-standard-assurance-engagements-isae-3402-assurance-reports-controls-service)** — Assurance reports on controls at service organizations. International equivalent of SOC 1.

## Sector Specific

### Automotive

- **[IATF 16949](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/)** — Quality management system for automotive production and relevant service part organizations. Replaces ISO/TS 16949. Required by most OEMs (Toyota, VW, GM, Ford, etc.).
- **[VDA 6.x](https://www.vda.de/en)** — German Automotive Industry Association quality standards series.
- **[ASPICE](https://vda-qmc.de/en/automotive-spice/)** — Automotive SPICE. Process assessment model for automotive software development.
- **[ISO 26262](https://www.iso.org/standard/68383.html)** — Functional safety for road vehicles (E/E systems).
- **[ISO/SAE 21434](https://www.iso.org/standard/70918.html)** — Cybersecurity engineering for road vehicles.

### Medical Devices

- **[ISO 13485](https://www.iso.org/standard/59752.html)** — Quality management system for medical devices. Required for market access in EU, Canada, and others.
- **[MDR / IVDR](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations_en)** — EU Medical Device Regulation (2017/745) and In Vitro Diagnostic Regulation (2017/746).
- **[FDA 21 CFR Part 820](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-system-qs-regulationmedical-device-good-manufacturing-practices)** — US FDA Quality System Regulation for medical device manufacturers.
- **[IEC 62304](https://www.iso.org/standard/38421.html)** — Medical device software lifecycle processes.
- **[ISO 14971](https://www.iso.org/standard/72704.html)** — Risk management for medical devices.

### Food Safety

- **[ISO 22000](https://www.iso.org/iso-22000-food-safety-management.html)** — Food Safety Management System (FSMS). Applies across the entire food chain.
- **[HACCP](https://www.fda.gov/food/guidance-regulation-food-and-dietary-supplements/hazard-analysis-critical-control-point-haccp)** — Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points. Systematic preventive approach to food safety.
- **[FSSC 22000](https://www.fssc.com/)** — Food Safety System Certification. Builds on ISO 22000, recognized by GFSI.
- **[BRC Global Standards](https://www.brcgs.com/)** — British Retail Consortium standards for food safety and packaging.
- **[IFS Food](https://www.ifs-certification.com/)** — International Featured Standard. European food retail standard.

### Aerospace

- **[AS9100](https://www.sae.org/standards/content/as9100d/)** — Quality Management System for Aviation, Space, and Defense organizations.
- **[DO-178C](https://www.rtca.org/content/do-178c)** — Software Considerations in Airborne Systems and Equipment Certification.
- **[DO-254](https://www.rtca.org/content/do-254)** — Design Assurance Guidance for Airborne Electronic Hardware.

### Software & IT Services

- **[ISO/IEC 20000-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/70636.html)** — IT Service Management System (ITSM). The certifiable standard underlying ITIL practices.
- **[ITIL 4](https://www.axelos.com/certifications/itil-service-management)** — IT Infrastructure Library. Best practice framework for IT service management. Organizations adopt it; individuals get certified in it.
- **[ISO/IEC 25010](https://www.iso.org/standard/35733.html)** — Systems and software quality models (SQuaRE series).
- **[ISO/IEC 12207](https://www.iso.org/standard/63712.html)** — Software lifecycle processes.
- **[ISO/IEC 15408](https://www.iso.org/standard/50341.html)** — Common Criteria for Information Technology Security Evaluation.

## Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

Frameworks helping organizations manage governance, enterprise risk, and regulatory compliance holistically.

- **[COBIT 2019](https://www.isaca.org/resources/cobit)** — Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies. Framework for IT governance and management by ISACA.
- **[COSO ERM](https://www.coso.org/)** — Committee of Sponsoring Organizations. Enterprise Risk Management integrated framework.
- **[ISO 31000](https://www.iso.org/iso-31000-risk-management.html)** — Risk Management. Principles, framework, and process for any organization. Not directly certifiable but widely referenced.
- **[OCEG GRC Capability Model](https://www.oceg.org/)** — Open Compliance and Ethics Group. The "Red Book" GRC framework.
- **[Basel III](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/basel3.htm)** — International regulatory framework for banks (capital requirements, stress testing).
- **[Solvency II](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/browse/regulation-and-policy/solvency-ii_en)** — EU regulatory framework for insurance companies.

## Gender Equality & Social Responsibility

- **[UNI PdR 125:2022](https://www.uni.com/pdr125/)** — Italian practice reference for gender equality certification in organizations. Basis for the Italian Gender Equality Certification ("Certificazione della Parità di Genere").
- **[ISO 30415](https://www.iso.org/standard/71164.html)** — Human resource management — Diversity and inclusion.
- **[SA8000](https://sa-intl.org/programs/sa8000/)** — Social Accountability International standard. Certifies ethical working conditions: no child labor, forced labor, discrimination; freedom of association.
- **[B Corp Certification](https://www.bcorporation.net/)** — Issued by B Lab. Certifies companies that meet high standards of social and environmental performance, accountability, and transparency.
- **[ISO 26000](https://www.iso.org/iso-26000-social-responsibility.html)** — Guidance on social responsibility. Not certifiable — provides guidance only.
